Before you send: three quick checks
  • Is the job still open? Roles marked Check it's still open weren't seen in the last few weeks. Open the job link first.
  • Is the email right? Addresses marked Likely, check first follow the company's usual format but weren't seen published. A LinkedIn message is the safer first step.
  • Is the person right? Confirmed means search results show them at the company in that job. Not confirmed means we couldn't check, not that it's wrong.
How this list was made
  1. Found open forward-deployed AI roles on careers pages and job boards, from eight angles at once.
  2. Researched each company: what its forward-deployed team does, who hires for it, and any email address it publishes.
  3. Checked each job, person and email again through search, and labelled what couldn't be confirmed.
  4. Scored each role 1 to 10 for you, wrote the messages, then had an editor check them against your real experience.

Nothing is sent for you. "Send email" opens your own email app, and the LinkedIn buttons copy the message and open the profile. Your progress, notes and edits are saved in this browser only.
