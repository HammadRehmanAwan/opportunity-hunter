OPPORTUNITY HUNTER· Forward Deployed AI Engineer

Scan → Filter → Enrich → Score → Gate → Outreach

Every open Forward Deployed AI Engineer role, the people behind it, and the message to send.

Built like the client-hunting pipeline: each opportunity is scored 1–10 with a rationale, comes with the hiring manager or recruiter to approach on LinkedIn, a verified or best-guess email, and a drafted email and LinkedIn note you can send with one click. Your approvals, notes and edits stay in this browser.

How this list was built

  1. Scan. Eight parallel searches over careers pages, Ashby / Greenhouse / Lever boards, LinkedIn Jobs, Otta, Wellfound and Work at a Startup for “forward deployed engineer” and adjacent titles.
  2. Filter. Only roles visible as open on a live page, de-duplicated by company and role.
  3. Enrich. Per company: what the forward-deployed team does, the hiring manager / recruiter / FDE lead with public LinkedIn profiles, any email the company or person has published, and the company's email pattern where it is documented.
  4. Verify. A second, sceptical pass re-fetched every job URL, re-checked every person's current title, and marked every email as published or guessed.
  5. Score. 1–10 against fit for a London-based applied-AI engineer, with a one-line rationale, a suggested contact and an outreach angle.
  6. Gate. You approve, contact or pass on each one. Status, notes and draft edits are saved in this browser and exportable as CSV.

Email addresses marked guess are derived from a documented company pattern and were not seen published anywhere. Verify before sending. Nothing on this page sends automatically: “Send email” opens your mail client, and the LinkedIn buttons copy the note and open the person's profile.

Data lives in data/opportunities.json. Re-run the research and rebuild with node scripts/build-data.mjs.
